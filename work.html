FINTECH • ALGO TRADING SUB-100MS EXECUTION

Nifty50 Automated Trading System

Professional-grade event-driven algorithmic execution engine built for extreme market volatility in Indian index derivatives.

The Challenge

Live market ticks generated massive throughput spikes. False breakout signals and emotional revenge trading caused drawdowns in manual testing.

The Architectural Solution

Engineered an asynchronous Node.js execution bot interfacing with Angel One SmartAPI via persistent WebSockets. Achieved sub-100ms order routing, zero missed ticks, and automated capital protection during 1,000+ simulated & live trade executions.

  • Node.js ES6+, WebSockets & Angel One SmartAPI

FINANCE • ENTERPRISE CRM 100% AUDITABLE

Realtime Financial Transaction & CRM Sync

High-concurrency webhook ingestion pipeline connecting third-party banking APIs with an internal customer relationship management system.

The Challenge

Spikes in payment gateway webhooks caused database locking, lost transaction states, and reconciliation delays between sales teams and accounting.

The Architectural Solution

Designed an idempotent event ingestion engine using Node.js and Redis streams. Transactions are acknowledged in under 15ms, buffered in in-memory queues, and reconciled asynchronously with full double-entry ledger verification.

The Verified Result

Processed over 250,000 monthly transactions without duplicate records or ledger imbalances. Decreased sales reconciliation lag from 4 hours to instantaneous.

  • Node.js Microservice & Redis Pub/Sub Stream Engine
  • PostgreSQL ACID-Compliant Transaction Journal
  • Idempotent Webhook Signatures with HMAC-SHA256
