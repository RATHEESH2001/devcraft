How Engagements Work

A transparent 4-stage engineering cycle with no guesswork or hourly billing surprises.

01

Discovery & System Audit

We examine your product requirements, existing architectural pain points, user flows, and scale goals. You receive a structured technical review without any initial obligation.

02

Technical Specification & Fixed Scope

I deliver an exact technical specification: API contracts, data models, infrastructure diagram, sprint schedule, and fixed-price quotation. No vague estimates.

03

Sprint Execution & Live Demos

Work is shipped in focused two-week sprints. Every sprint concludes with working, tested software deployed to a staging environment for your feedback.

04

We execute zero-downtime cutover with automated backups and telemetry. Full documentation, test suites, and git repository ownership are transferred to your team.
